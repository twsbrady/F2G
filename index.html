Inside the injector

What the additive
puts back.

Fyrex CI is the additive we dose into every litre of diesel we deliver. Taking the sulphur out of modern diesel took much of its natural lubricity with it. Two minutes on what Fyrex CI does inside a high-pressure injection system, and why that matters for your fleet.
